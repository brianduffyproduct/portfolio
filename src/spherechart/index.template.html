Built by Brian Duffy. Positions are geocentric and tropical, computed with Astronomy Engine (good to about an arcminute). Stars and constellation figures come from the d3-celestial catalogue. The sky view ignores atmospheric refraction. Paths through the zodiac are solid for the past and dashed for the future. A chart places each planet by its degree along the ecliptic, so a body that sits well off the ecliptic (the Moon, Pluto) can look like it is in a neighbouring Placidus house in the sky view.
