About

I don't think I've met anyone who has had a "normal" path to becoming a Product Manager. I went to art school to learn how to make cartoons. After I graduated, I ended up making some cartoons for a software startup, and they started asking me if I could make banner ads, landing pages, and user interfaces, and it turned out that I could.

I kept making cartoons, eventually becoming an animation producer at a small independent studio, shepherding segments for documentary films and episodes of NOVA on PBS. After over a decade of the freelance life, I decided to pivot to full-time work, joining SimplePractice as a support specialist in 2018 when it was a startup with 85 employees.

I stood out for my constant Slack messages about bugs I found and opportunities I noticed from my onboarding calls with new customers. I learned about product management by directly collaborating with the Product team, and was encouraged to apply internally to an Associate PM position. This led to a full Product Manager position, where I was responsible for all of SimplePractice's communication features, from secure messaging to Telehealth video to multiple special projects. One of them, a feature I took from idea to launch, was adopted by about half of eligible customers within four months.

I'm currently seeking new opportunities, either by continuing in healthcare SaaS or by applying my product skills to other industries.
